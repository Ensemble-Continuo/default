# About Us

Ensemble Continuo is an adult classical chamber choir based in Palo Alto, California. The choir is committed to artistic excellence: performing challenging, diverse, and rarely-performed works.

Founded in 2023, the choir has established a vibrant presence in the Bay Area. With about 32 singers ranging in age from 16–65, Ensemble Continuo comprises singers from all backgrounds brought together by their passion for classical repertoire. In a 2026 review, [San Francisco Classical Voice said of the ensemble][SFCV Martin review], “It is rare to hear a performance of such artistry from a community choir.”

Ensemble Continuo seeks to:

- Provide a welcoming environment for experienced singers to grow and connect, offering artistic development through high-caliber instruction, voice coaching, and challenging repertoire.
- Champion diverse and rarely-performed works, and to collaborate with outstanding local musicians to enrich the cultural landscape of the Bay Area.
- Ensure accessibility by offering affordable ticket prices for audiences.

Ensemble Continuo has been invited to perform across the Bay Area, including at the Summer Sacred Music Series at Stanford Memorial Church, the International Performing Arts of America Festival at California Theater in San Jose, and even as onboard carolers for Caltrain’s Holiday Train in 2025. In June 2025, the choir embarked on its first tour to perform works by Poulenc and Bernstein at Chicago's Holy Name Cathedral. The choir also hosts an annual *Messiah* sing-along for the local community.

Ensemble Continuo is currently the choir-in-residence at All Saints’ Episcopal Church. For our 2026–2027 season, we will be performing works from French composers in the fall and Bach’s *St. John Passion* around Easter. Details and ticket information can be found [here](/performances).

## About Our Musical Director

Founder and musical director of Ensemble Continuo, Christopher Pluntke, graduated from the Hochschule für Musik Würzburg in 2007 and has 25 years of experience leading vocal ensembles, period instrument groups, and performing as organist, pianist and continuo player. After obtaining his PhD at UCL, he moved to the Bay Area, where he has been active as conductor, pianist, and organist. He has served as music director and organist at All Saints' Episcopal Church in Palo Alto since January 2025.
